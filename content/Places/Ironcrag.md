Ironcrag is the predominantly Orc society on [[Riftspire]].

- **Jagged Peaks**: The Ironcrag banner bears a crude depiction of black mountains or crags, symbolizing resilience and the strength of the land itself.
- **Fortresses**: Their strongholds are carved into the foothills, reinforced with dark iron plating and adorned with trophies of battle.
- **Colors**: Deep red and black, representing blood, strength, and unrelenting determination.
- **Societal Values**: Ironcrag Orcs prize individual strength and cunning, with a loose hierarchy where warleaders earn their positions through trial by combat.
- **Military Focus**: Their warriors, the **Cragfangs**, use brutal, close-combat tactics, leveraging the terrain and ambush strategies. They are masters of warbands and skirmishes in the foothills.
