Solvaris is the predominantly human society on the island of [[Riftspire]]


- **Sun Iconography**: The Solvarian banner features a radiant golden sun, symbolizing light, hope, and their perceived moral superiority.
- **Architecture**: Gleaming spires and white stone fortresses dominate the human settlements, with intricate carvings depicting tales of their ancestors rising to claim the skies.
- **Colors**: Gold and white, representing purity and triumph.
