## History

Created by [[Archmage Elara Nightshade]], it now serves as the main travel network for the more well-off citizens of Aetheria. It simply requires a small "donation" of 10 GP to the [[The Institute of Mystical Studies]] (per person, of course) to be able to traverse the portals. Thanks to its easy of use and popularity, it has brought a massive cashflow into [[The Institute of Mystical Studies]].
