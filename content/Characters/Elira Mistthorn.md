## History

One of the 4 members of [[Breakers of Mist]], she was selected thanks to her excellent tracking and survival skills. Unfortunately, she was lost within the mist, separated from [[Archmage Elara Nightshade]] and [[Ormuk Ironstride]]. She was with [[Malachi the Fallen]] as he began to go mad, and she tried to escape. It's assumed she succumbed to the mist, but it is unconfirmed if she is alive or dead.
