## History

Malachi was original a well respected Sorcerer. He was contracted by [[Orion]] to work in tandem with [[Archmage Elara Nightshade]] to try and determine the Mist's properties. Upon his arrival to [[Mistveil]] with [[Breakers of Mist]], he was quickly consumed by his lack of magical abilities. He felt as though his very marrow had been stripped away from him. He began to quickly descend in to madness, causing the party to split. [[Archmage Elara Nightshade]] and [[Ormuk Ironstride]] were able to successfully make it back to the [[Airships]] that they arrived on, whereas [[Elira Mistthorn]] fled deeper into the mist. 

Malachi eventually was able to climb the treacherous mountain on [[Mistveil]], and was able to summon the smallest spark of magic. He cast a powerful enough dimension door to send him anywhere but there, and he found himself within a quite glade. His innate magical ability rushed back to him, and he was consumed by it's power. He swore vengeance on the mist for taking away something that was so precious to him, and he began foul experiments to increase his magical abilities. 

He continued to spiral into madness, and summoned a horde of the undead to conquer the island he arrived on. His goal was to dominate all of the magic within this realm, to rival even that of the mists power.
