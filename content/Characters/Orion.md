Orion was a powerful Wizard, and is mostly known for his founding of [[The Institute of Mystical Studies]] after discovering the powerful connection to the Weave on [[Weaveward]]. He also was the individual who commissioned the creation of [[Breakers of Mist]], an adventuring party devoted to discovering the mysteries of [[Mistveil]] and hopefully dispelling the mist.
