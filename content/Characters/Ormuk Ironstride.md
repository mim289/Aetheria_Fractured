## Backstory

Famed for his bravery and combat prowess, he was one of the founding members of [[Breakers of Mist]]. He was able to withstand the mist and make it out alive, although he came back a changed man. He moved to [[Ironheart]] upon his return form [[Mistveil]], laid down his sword, and began a simple life of working as a combat instructor for new adventurers. His great-grandson would eventually found [[Ironstride Adventuring Co.]] in his honor.
