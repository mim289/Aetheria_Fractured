# Welcome to the world of Aetheria!

This realm is one made up of large floating islands in the sky. They are varied and diverse, due to the large period of time before the Nexus was created. There are hundreds of small islands speckling the sky, most too small to have too much of note. 

The larger ones, though, are all united via the Nexus; a weave-based portal network created by [[Archmage Elara Nightshade]]. For a small "donation" of a few gold pieces, citizens are able to traverse these portals, quickly jumping from Island to Island. The main exception to this is the island of [[Mistveil]], the largest of the island world, but one shrouded in a magical suppressing fog. 

For islands unable to be linked via the Nexus, or too small or unremarkable, the use of [[Airships]] is common.

## The Mechanical Tidbits

This particular adventure is for players starting at level 3. You are all contracted currently with the [[Ironstride Adventuring Co.]] and have up to this point done some small adventures. A little giant rat slaying here, a little "Get me some herbs from [[Verdance]]" there! 

We will discuss if the party up to this point have been solo-adventurers, or if you have formed a party already.

We are also using the 2024 ruleset, and will be playing on Roll20 if we need a map!

## Character Origin Inspiration

This section is to serve as some jumping off point for where your character may have come from, and some of their life story!

#### Locations

- For those of you who feel like you've come from the big city, check out [[Ironheart]]! It's a city of industry and commerce, but it has a lot of dark alleyways for those who feel like the path of "good" isn't always the right path. The thieves guild is prominent here, but so is the main adventurers guild, [[Ironstride Adventuring Co.]]!
- Maybe your character is one with nature, feeling right at home amongst the towering trees, singing birds, and ever-shifting wildlife. If so, check out [[Verdance]], a lush rainforest island with several village communities strewn throughout it!
- Some of you may just feel like a day at the beach (or under the water) is where your character feels called to. Check out [[The Wake]], a giant ocean island! Whole underwater civilizations reside there!
- War, war never changes... and if you LOVE that fact, maybe you feel at home on [[Riftspire]]! This island has long been at war, divided by two major kingdoms: [[Ironcrag]] and [[Solvaris]]. A tentative peace has just been struck, thanks to [[Archmage Elara Nightshade]], but that does little to quell the generations of hatred within the populace hearts...
- Perhaps your character comes from a place where light and happiness are only rumors on the wind. If a land of necromancy, decay, and death sound like home, check out [[Malachi's Embrace]]! Ruled over by the Lich King [[Malachi the Fallen]], the humans who once called this home have been corrupted, turning into something new!
- You awake to the sound of hundreds of church bells all tolling at once, and instead of a headache, you welcome it as the sounds of home. If divine light and warmth is your idea of homey, check out [[Celestial Crest]]!
- The smell of musty old books is the first thing your character remembers. The sounds of people practicing Fire Bolts against a stone wall, people chanting ritual spells, or simply getting "SHHHHH"'d within [[The Institute of Mystical Studies]] libraries are sounds of home, then maybe [[Weaveward]] is calling you! Maybe your parents were professors, or some college romance!

#### Career/Class

- Bards and Rogues alike can strike it big in [[Ironheart]], where taverns are plentiful and pockets are full!
- Barbarians and Fighters would probably feel right at home on the battlefields of [[Riftspire]]
- Clerics and Paladins will have to have spent at least a little bit of time within [[Celestial Crest]] communing with their chosen Diety!
- Druids and Rangers will probably have spent a decent amount of time amongst the trees of [[Verdance]]
- Wizards, Warlocks, and maybe the occasional overachieving Sorcerer or Bard have most likely spent a significant amount of time studying at [[The Institute of Mystical Studies]] on [[Weaveward]]
- Monks could possibly find enlightenment either within the divine light of [[Celestial Crest]] or among the battlefields of [[Riftspire]].
- Any other 5e classes are welcome! Just chat with me and we can work through it!
