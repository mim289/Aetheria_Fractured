## History

One of the largest islands in Aetheria, it is home to two long-warring kingdoms, who have just recently brokered peace. The two kingdoms, [[Solvaris]] and [[Ironcrag]], are situated on opposite sides of the island, with large plains and foothills separating the two. There is a big stretch of land on the borders of each nation deemed "unlivable" by civilians of both nations, due to the long history of raiding parties attacking villages on the outskirts of the kingdom. 

## Geography

The natural landscape is predominantly plains and and foothills dotted with small villages of Orcs and Humans. On opposite sides of the Island sit two large capitals.
