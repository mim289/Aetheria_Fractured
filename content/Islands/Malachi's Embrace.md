The island that [[Malachi the Fallen]] conquered after his decent to madness.  

## History

The original name of this island has been lost, and it's now known simply as Malachi's Embrace. Before Malachi escaped [[Mistveil]] and ascended to becoming the Lich King of this island, it was a small and secluded island within Aetheria. Lush green grasslands gave way to snow covered peaks that were visible well before the rest of the island was. Several small towns of humans littered the country side. 

After [[Malachi the Fallen]]'s decent into Lichdom, he traveled to this island and was able to easily conquered it. He set up his citadel at the foot of the mountains, and raised an army of the undead. He spread his necrotic magic throughout the core of the island, corrupting it. The lush grasslands fell away to ash, and the humans there began changing. Unable to escape the island, their choice was only to serve [[Malachi the Fallen]], either willfully in life, or forcefully through death. 

The people who bent the knee to [[Malachi the Fallen]] willingly started to slowly see changes in their form. Their skin began to pale, their hair all shifted to black, and their eye colors faded into obsidian.
