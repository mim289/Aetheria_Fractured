## History

A giant floating island covered entirely by an ocean. Little is known about this island, aside from the occasional Triton coming to shore to greet guests. This is one of the few major islands that does not have a Nexus connection, as the water-dwelling people felt no need to leave their island. 

People local to here are mostly Triton, Sea Elves, and Water Genasi. There is a local legend that if someone were brave enough to drill a hole in the bottom of the ocean, then a waterfall would be made that would gently carry all of the inhabitants to the "True Depths".

## Geography

Predominantly covered in oceans, there is a large mountain in the middle.
