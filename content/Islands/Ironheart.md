## History

Heavily industrialized thanks to the native Gnome population, this island is virtually entirely covered in the city that shares it's name. Ironheart City serves as a major historical commercial hub of Aetheria, being the first "crossroads" uniting the world thanks to their invention of the [[Airships]]. The city also has vast industrial zones, from metal refinement, blacksmithing, and of course the Aerodrome, where the [[Airships]] are created. 

Being seen as a major opportunity for riches and a better life, people from all races and regions have moved into Ironheart City. Various cultural neighborhoods have been established, and it's viewed as a melting pot. 

There are whispers from locals to steer clear of the alleys in the Iron Quarter. Rumor has it that there is an outbreak of lycanthropy, but no official statement has been made. 

## Geography

What used to be rolling hills that were rich in iron has now been flattened and entirely consumed by Ironheart City.
