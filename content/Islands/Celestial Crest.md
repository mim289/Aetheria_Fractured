## History

Some say that Lathander himself descended down to the Celestial Crest, gracing it with his benevolent light so that all who may wander may find their way there. The Crest is home to temples to a multitude of deities, and serves as a central point of pilgrimages. It is also known for educating Paladins and Clerics of every denomination, commissioning them out into the rest of Aetheria to protect the innocent.
