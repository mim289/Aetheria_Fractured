## History

Little is known about this island. It is the largest island in all of Aetheria, but is enshrouded by a magic-nullifying mist. Everyone who has attempted to go to Mistveil has either not come back, or has been drastically changed by the experience. The only notable figures to make it out in one piece have been [[Archmage Elara Nightshade]] and [[Ormuk Ironstride]].
