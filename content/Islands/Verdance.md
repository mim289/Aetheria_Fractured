## History

Verdance is known for its dense, lush rainforests. 

## Geography
Verdance is a colossal floating island suspended in the sky, wreathed in clouds that glisten with morning dew. Its heart is a sprawling rainforest, alive with color and motion. Towering kapok trees dominate the skyline, their sprawling canopies forming a green ocean above the world. Vines heavy with flowers in iridescent hues cascade like waterfalls, while bioluminescent fungi illuminate shadowy groves in vibrant blues and purples. Streams of crystalline water wind through the underbrush, tumbling over cliffs in shimmering waterfalls that dissipate into mist before they touch the clouds below. The air is thick with the scent of rain-soaked earth and the hum of unseen creatures.

Gigantic ferns and luminous orchids thrive beneath the canopy, while smaller glades are lit by golden sunbeams filtering through the leaves. Floating motes of pollen drift lazily in the air, glittering like stars in the humid atmosphere. Winged creatures with feathers and scales dart between trees, their cries echoing like flutes.

Massive cliffs along the island's edge are lined with woven rope bridges and wooden platforms, where wind-catchers spin and sway, harnessing the perpetual breezes. From these vantage points, Verdance offers awe-inspiring vistas of endless sky and drifting clouds, tinged with the pink and gold of eternal sunrise.

## Tribes
- **Aarakocra**  
    Skyborn hunters and guardians of the winds, the Aarakocra revere Verdance as a sacred roost. Their airy cliffside villages are adorned with wind chimes and feathered totems. They are skilled in aerial combat and weaving, creating flowing tapestries that catch the island’s endless breezes.
    
- **Tabaxi**  
    Agile and curious, the Tabaxi make their homes deep within the rainforest. These feline humanoids are expert climbers, moving effortlessly through the treetops. Their culture revolves around storytelling and artistry, with their woven goods and carvings reflecting the vibrant flora and fauna of Verdance.
    
- **Wood Elves**  
    Guardians of the rainforest, wood elves live in harmony with the land. Their treetop cities are camouflaged with living vegetation, blending seamlessly into the canopy. They practice ancient druidic rituals, ensuring the rainforest thrives while defending it from any harm.
    
- **Lizardfolk**  
    Lizardfolk dwell in the misty riverbanks and hidden caves of Verdance. Known for their cunning and resilience, they craft tools and weapons from the rainforest's abundant resources. Their connection to the island's water sources gives them a spiritual bond with Verdance’s lifeblood.
    
- **Firbolg**  
    Towering yet gentle, the Firbolg are caretakers of the rainforest's deepest glades. They cultivate symbiotic relationships with the flora and fauna, acting as shepherds for rare plants and creatures. Their wisdom and connection to nature make them revered among the other tribes.
